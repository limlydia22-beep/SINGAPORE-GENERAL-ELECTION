Singapore General Election Results

GE2015 · GE2020 · GE2025 — Parliamentary general elections

Source: Elections Department Singapore (ELD), “Parliamentary General Election Results by Candidate”, published on data.gov.sg. Official results: eld.gov.sg.

Vote shares are each party's share of valid votes in the constituencies it contested, added up nationally; walkovers (no vote) are counted for seats only. National totals here are calculated from the per-constituency figures and may differ very slightly from ELD's published national figures because of rounding. This is an unofficial app: please check important figures against ELD.
